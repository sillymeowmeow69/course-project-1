Course Project Specification

Fictional Story:
Victor used to work as a voice actor and now wants to continue his passion privately as a freelancer.
But he is missing the publicity, so i make him a website.

Title of site: Victor's Voicing

Target Audience: Primary (the people who pay):

Indie game studio producers and audio leads — character voices, barks, in-world narration.
Animation and motion-graphics producers — explainer films, shorts, title reads.
Ad agency creatives and radio producers — 15/30-second spots.
E-learning and corporate video producers — long-form narration.

Secondary: Friends of Victor for fun

Purpose of the site: 
make sure people know about victor and his previous work and serve a convenient interface to get into contact for business related work
