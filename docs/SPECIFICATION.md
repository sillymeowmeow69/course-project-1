Course Project Specification
============================

Title of site
-------------
Victor's Voicing


Customer
--------
- Victor, voice actor, Helsinki.
- Worked privately for several voice acting companies, 2021 to 2024. Three years.
- Mainly adult animation (hentai).
- Now continues as a freelancer, because he enjoys it.
- Not his income. A hobby that might turn useful.
- No stress about the career, so no pressure on the site either.


Starting point
--------------
- Nothing online yet. He only just picked it up again.
- No Instagram, no old site, nothing to migrate or link to.
- This site is his first public presence.


Target audience
---------------
- Primary: indie game studios, animation producers, ad and e-learning producers.
- Secondary: friends of Victor, for fun.


Purpose of the site
-------------------
- Tell people who Victor is and what he has done.
- Let them hear him.
- Give them two ways to get in touch: the booking form, or email.


Done when
---------
- All pages are up and validate.
- Three paid enquiries come in through the site.


Out of scope
------------
- Online payment.
- Login area, user accounts.
- Blog.
- Finnish or Swedish translations of the site.
- A CMS. Victor cannot edit the text himself; changes go through the developer.
- Booking calendar integration.
- Anything beyond booking and information about Victor.


Service and pricing
-------------------
- Prices are agreed individually per job, nothing fixed on the site.
- Rough estimate for orientation: 100 to 200 EUR for a short job,
  around 8 EUR per finished minute for longer narration.
- Replies within two or three days at the latest.
- Audio back within three working days of the script arriving.
- Two or three takes per line. If none fit, the next round is free.
- Recordings in English. Direction in English, Finnish or Swedish.


Where he records
----------------
- Victor records at his own place. His address is not published.
- Cornern, Roomankatu 5, 00560 Helsinki is only the meeting point if the
  customer asks for a session in person.
- There are no opening hours. A time is agreed privately.
- Most customers never visit. They send a script and receive audio.


Responsiveness
--------------
- Mobile and desktop first. Tablet and 4K supported.
- No smartwatch. No 8K.
- Three breakpoints: 768px (tablet), 1024px (desktop nav), 1600px (large and 4K).
- No JavaScript anywhere, so the site also works with scripting disabled.


Visual design
-------------
- Dark theme. Warm amber light in a dark booth.
- Borrows from studio hardware: engraved labels, level meters, energy in the audio.

| Token      | Hex     | Use                          |
|------------|---------|------------------------------|
| --ink      | #15120F | page background, warm black  |
| --booth    | #211C17 | panels, cards                |
| --cream    | #F3EBE0 | body text                    |
| --amber    | #F2A93B | accent, headings, hover      |
| --amber-dim| #A87527 | pressed and inactive amber   |

- Display font: Oswald. Condensed, loud, fits a stage name.
- Body font: Inter. Plain and readable.
- Mono font: JetBrains Mono. Used for small engraved-looking labels.


Pages
-----
- index.html - hero, featured picture, what he records, call to action
- about.html - background, the short version
- demos.html - voice reels, video, picture gallery in an iframe
- contact.html - email, reply time, map of the meeting point
- booking.html - the booking form
- specifications.html - this specification
- report.html - the web report
- gallery.html - the thumbnails, loaded inside the iframe on demos.html
