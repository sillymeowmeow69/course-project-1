Specification

What was agreed

The plan this site was built against. Written before the code, so that finished means something.

The customer

  • Victor, voice actor, Helsinki.
  • Worked privately for several voice acting companies from 2021 to 2024. Three years.
  • Mainly adult animation.
  • Continues now as a freelancer, because he enjoys it.
  • This is not his income. It is a hobby that might turn useful.

Starting point

  • Nothing online yet. He only just picked it up again.
  • No Instagram, no old site, nothing to migrate.
  • This site is his first public presence, so it has to carry everything on its own.

Target audience

  • Indie game studios, animation producers, ad and e-learning producers.
  • Friends of Victor, for fun.

Purpose and success

  • Tell people who Victor is and what he has done.
  • Let them hear him.
  • Give them two ways in: the booking form, or an email.

The site counts as a success once three paid enquiries have come in through it. That is the number we agreed to measure against, not a feeling about the design.

Out of scope

Written down so it cannot be asked for later without a new agreement:

  • Online payment.
  • Login area or user accounts.
  • A blog.
  • Finnish or Swedish translations of the site.
  • A booking calendar.
  • A content management system.

Why Victor cannot edit the text himself

The pages are written by hand in HTML and CSS. There is no editing interface behind them, because a content management system was not in scope and would have cost more than the whole site. In practice that means Victor sends the new wording and the developer puts it in.

This is a deliberate trade. A hand-written site is small, fast and has nothing to keep updated for security. The price is that text changes are not self-service.

How we worked

Specification first
This document was agreed before any code was written. It is what the result is measured against.
Mobile first
Every layout was built for the narrow screen first, then widened. It is easier to add space than to take it away.
Version control
Every change is a commit in Git, so there is a dated history of what changed and a way back if something breaks.
Small steps
One page at a time, reviewed in the browser before moving on, instead of one big reveal at the end.

Service terms

  • Prices are agreed per job. Nothing fixed is published on the site.
  • As a rough orientation: around 100 to 200 euro for a short job, around 8 euro per finished minute for longer narration.
  • Replies within two or three days at the latest.
  • Audio back within three working days of the script arriving.
  • Two or three takes per line. If none fit, the next round is free.
  • Recordings in English. Direction in English, Finnish or Swedish.

Where he records

  • Victor records at his own place. His address is not published anywhere on the site.
  • Cornern, Roomankatu 5 in Helsinki, is only the meeting point if a customer asks for a session in person.
  • There are no opening hours. A time is agreed privately.
  • Most customers never visit. They send a script and receive audio.

Responsiveness, and its limits

Promised, and tested:

Device Width Supported
Phone, portrait from 360px Yes, this is the starting point
Tablet from 768px Yes
Laptop and desktop from 1024px Yes, the menu unfolds here
Large screen and 4K from 1600px Yes, wider measure and larger type
Smartwatch under 300px No, out of scope
8K beyond 4K No, out of scope

Three breakpoints, at 768, 1024 and 1600 pixels. There is no JavaScript on the site at all, so it also works with scripting switched off. Internet Explorer is not supported.

Type choices

Oswald
Headings, menu and buttons. Condensed and loud, the way a stage name should look.
Inter
Body text. Plain, modern and readable at small sizes.
JetBrains Mono
Small labels. Reads like text engraved on a mixing desk.

Site map

Solid arrows are the main menu, which sits on every page. Dashed boxes are reached from inside a page or from the footer.

Layout plan

Every page is built from the same three parts, so the site feels like one place:

  • Header with the logo on the left and the menu on the right. It sticks to the top while you scroll. Under 1024 pixels the menu folds into a button.
  • Main as a stack of sections. Each one opens with a small label, then a heading, then the content. Sections are separated by a thin rule rather than by boxes.
  • Footer with the booking links, the project links and a back to top link.

The home page is the only one with a two-column hero. Everything else uses the same single column that splits into two at the tablet breakpoint.

What finished means

  • Seven pages, all with a title, a heading, text and links.
  • Audio, video, a picture gallery and a map embedded.
  • Every page passing the W3C HTML validator, every stylesheet passing the CSS validator.
  • The web report written as one of the pages.
