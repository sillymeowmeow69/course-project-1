How I work

“Send me the script. You get the audio back the same week.”

Victor

Every job starts with the script. Send it over as plain text or a document, tell me who the character is and roughly how fast you want them to talk, and I will read you two or three takes so you can pick the one that fits the cut. If none of them land, the next round is free.

I record in a treated booth inside Cornern, so the raw files come back clean and there is nothing to edit out of the room. Files are delivered as WAV for mixing, and as MP3 if you only need something to drop into a timeline for a first pass.

I read in English. I can take direction in Finnish or Swedish, which covers most of the producers I work with here, but the finished recordings are English only.
